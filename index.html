MODES Lab · Research Lab at SWEN

Model-Driven Engineering & Software Mining

The MODES Laboratory is proposed within one of the most active international research groups in Model-Driven Engineering. MODES expands the group's research toward recommender systems for software engineering, with a special focus on modeling.

In particular, the lab explores and develops advanced intelligent model assistants that combine MDE techniques with software-mining and modern machine learning methods. Applications include technical debt detection, energy efficiency of model transformations, LLMs for modeling, and cloud-based modeling platforms.

Bio

Alfonso Pierantonio is Full Professor of Computer Science at the University of L'Aquila and the coordinator of SWEN. He is a member of the MODES Laboratory, where he works on model-driven engineering, domain-specific languages, and the co-evolution of models and metamodels.

MOSAICO

Multi-agent Orchestration for Software AI Collaboration.

Period

since January 2025

SWEN Lab

MODES Lab

Project site

mosaico-project.eu ↗

MOSAICO is a Horizon Europe project advancing the use of AI multi-agent systems for software engineering tasks. The project investigates how orchestrated collaboration among specialized AI agents — each with different tooling, knowledge, and reasoning capabilities — can improve software development activities such as coding, testing, refactoring, and documentation.

The SWEN contribution, via the MODES Lab, focuses on model-driven approaches for specifying agent roles, exchange protocols, and the coordination of agent workflows, as well as the empirical evaluation of multi-agent collaboration patterns in real software-engineering settings.

TRex-SE

Trustworthy Recommenders x Software Engineers.

Period

Nov 2022 – Nov 2025

SWEN Lab

MODES Lab

Project site

trexse-prin.github.io ↗

TRex-SE aims to develop methods to improve the trustworthiness of Recommender Systems for Software Engineering (RSSEs). The project's contributions target three dimensions:

  1. The development of vulnerability and privacy-aware miners able to produce training sets for RSSEs that do not embed security or confidentiality risks;
  2. The conceptualization and instantiation of a trustworthiness model for RSSEs, with concrete quality properties and measurement protocols;
  3. The definition and development of trustworthy RSSEs that leverage this model and capture developers' feedback during development.

HALO

Human-aligned autonomy of AI systems.

Period

Sep 2023 – Sep 2025

Funding

PNRR

Status

Active

HALO empowers users to adjust the autonomy of AI systems to align with their ethical preferences. It proposes a dynamic software exoskeleton that allows users to express moral preferences — such as privacy or dignity — and to control how autonomy is distributed across AI components, software agents, and human users.

The approach combines software engineering primitives with ethical reasoning at runtime, so that the system's level of autonomy is not hard-coded but becomes a design parameter that each user (and each use case) can modulate.

BeT

Behavior-enabled IoT.

Period

Sep 2023 – Sep 2025

SWEN Lab

SoSy Group

Funding

PRIN 2022

BeT (Behavior-enabled IoT) focuses on improving the design and implementation of Internet of Behaviors (IoB) systems, which have emerged from the increasing integration of IoT, AI, and human-centered technologies. IoB involves collecting and analyzing human behaviors through IoT networks or social platforms to inform and influence user actions.

The SWEN contribution, via the SoSy Group, focuses on the formal composition and coordination of behavior-aware components, protocols for negotiating behavioural adaptations, and architectural patterns for IoB applications.

2020W3A5FY

EMELIOT

Engineered Machine Learning-intensive IoT systems.

Period

Jun 2022 – Jun 2025

SWEN Lab

SoSy Group

Funding

PRIN 2020

EMELIOT studies solutions for engineering highly-dependable, ML-intensive IoT systems. The project focuses on systems where sensors collect data from physical devices, while actuators can change their state based on the collected data.

Collected data are filtered, aggregated, and used to manage ML models either locally on the edge (via dedicated IoT gateways) or on remote cloud servers. EMELIOT develops methods for guaranteeing dependability properties — reliability, availability, safety — across these heterogeneous, ML-driven distributed architectures.

P2022SELA7

RECHARGE

Automated performance testing in CI/CD via static analysis and search-based algorithms.

Period

Nov 2023 – Nov 2025

SWEN Lab

SPENCER Lab

Project site

recharge-project.github.io ↗

The RECHARGE project addresses the challenge of automating performance testing in Continuous Integration and Deployment pipelines by leveraging static analysis and search-based algorithms. The project proposes a novel framework to automate performance testing in CI/CD by introducing automated performance monitoring, which combines static analysis, regression testing optimization, and CI/CD processes to track software performance efficiently over time.

Software & Tools

Research we ship

SWEN actively develops and maintains academic software used in research and education worldwide. Our tools span model-driven engineering, software performance analysis, software architecture, and recommender systems for developers. Contributions are welcome.

Category: Language:
MDE Framework Active

Jjodel

A cloud-based reflective modeling platform designed to reduce the cognitive complexity of MDE tools. Supports real-time collaboration, live metamodel + model co-evolution, and syntax customization — with zero-setup access from the browser.

TypeScript · Web MODES Lab
jjodel.io ↗ Paper (MODELS'23) ↗
Performance Suite Active · v1.3.0

JMT — Java Modelling Tools

Open-source suite for queueing network modelling, discrete-event simulation, and workload analysis. Six applications covering performance evaluation, mean value analysis, bottleneck identification, and teaching. Originally developed at Politecnico di Milano, now maintained under SWEN supervision.

Java · GPL SPENCER Lab
sourceforge.net ↗ Bertoli, Casale, Serazzi · 2009

Our commitment

Research software is a first-class output

We believe tools and datasets are as important as papers. All our software is open-source (most under permissive licenses), actively maintained, and documented. Contributions, bug reports, and academic collaborations are welcome — see each tool's repository for contribution guidelines.
